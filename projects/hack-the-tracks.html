Hack the Tracks

Python Genetic Algorithm Optimization Streamlit

36 Hours to Glory

What happens when you lock a team called "Biology Meets Rigi" in a room for 36 hours? Apparently, we build an award-winning optimization engine. We dove into the "Hack the Tracks" challenge—organized by the Railway Talent Hub and backed by Switzerland Innovation—with ambition and focus. Our goal? Solve the SBB's nightmare of coordinating construction sites across all modes of transport.

Genetic Algorithms & Good Vibes

Our weapon of choice? A genetic algorithm. Instead of relying on standard heuristics, our code was evolving. We built a full-stack solution that mutated and crossed-over schedules to find the perfect balance, resolving conflicts faster than a human ever could. We wrapped this complex logic in a Python backend and slapped a sleek Streamlit frontend on it. And yes, we even managed to get some sleep!

Taking Home the Gold

The result? Our automated planner didn't just work; it impressed. We secured 1st place and the grand prize of 25,000 CHF! Proof that even "Biology" majors (and engineers) can revolutionize infrastructure planning given enough pressure.
