BetterContacts

Android Kotlin Graph Visualization UI/UX

The Concept

Contacts apps have been boring lists for too long. BetterContacts changes that by visualizing your network as it truly exists: a web of connections.

Instead of an alphabetical list, you see yourself in the center, surrounded by your friends, family, and colleagues. Groups are color-coded, and connections between people are clearly visible.

Contextual Memory: The app is built to help you remember people and details about them. If you recognize a face or know a person from a specific context (like "Uni" or "Volleyball") but forgot their name, simply look at the relevant group to find them instantly.

Features

  • Grouping: Contacts are grouped by affiliation (Work, Family, University).
  • Visual Relationships: See who knows whom and remember where you know people from.
  • Voice to text: To add additional information about a contact, you can simply speak and the app will use whisper from openAI to convert your voice to text.
