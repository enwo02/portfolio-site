Snake: The Assembly Challenge 🐍

Assembly FPGA Drivers Microcontrollers

Why write a game in a high-level language when you can manually manage every single register? For our Microcontrollers course, we decided to ignore the easy path and write Snake entirely in Assembly.

The result? A glorious 8x8 LED matrix display, an LCD for high scores (so your shame persists), and controls that are... unique. You can guide the snake with a standard IR remote, or if you're feeling magical, wave your hands over distance sensors like a low-budget Jedi. It features custom drivers and interrupt handling that we wrote from scratch—because libraries are for the weak.
