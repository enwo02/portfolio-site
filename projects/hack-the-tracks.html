The Hackathon

In a 36-hour sprint, our team participated in the "Hack the Tracks" challenge. Our innovative solution for optimizing construction site planning between different transport modes secured us the 1st place and a prize of 25,000 CHF.

The Solution

We developed a full-stack solution featuring a genetic algorithm to optimize complex scheduling constraints. The system manages conflicts and dependencies between various transport mode construction sites (rail, road, gondola).

The implementation included a robust backend for the optimization logic and a user-friendly Streamlit frontend for visualization and interaction.

Success

Our team "Biology Meets Rigi" successfully pitched the solution, demonstrating real-time optimization capabilities and a clear path to impact.
