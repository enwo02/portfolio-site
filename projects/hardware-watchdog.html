Implementation of a hardware watchdog

SystemVerilog RISC-V FPGA Hardware Verification

The Mission

This project was part of the VLSI 2 course at ETH Zurich. The goal? Take the CROC SoC (a lightweight RISC-V SoC) and make it bulletproof. We implemented a hardware watchdog that monitors the system and, upon detecting a freeze or deadlock, hits the reset button faster than you can say "segfault".

The Results

Does it work? Absolutely. We threw everything we had at it.

  • 32/32 Randomized Tests Passed: Bulletproof validation.
  • Simulated Reset Behavior: Confirmed correct recovery in RTL simulations.
  • Physical Design: DRC and LVS Clean. Zero violations.

By the Numbers

  • Watchdog Core Area: ~3859 µm² (Tiny! Only 0.5% of i_croc)
  • Total Area (with Wrapper): ~8582 µm² (Still just 1.1% of i_croc)
  • Power Impact: Negligible.
