EPFL Spacecraft Team

PCB Design Leadership Space Systems Electronics

Start of an Era

The EPFL Spacecraft Team is a student association at EPFL dedicated to designing, building, and launching satellites. I joined the team with a passion for space systems and soon took on a leadership role.

My Role

As a Team Leader, I managed a group of students working on the critical electronic subsystems of the satellite. My responsibilities included verifying designs, coordinating between subsystems, and ensuring we met our strict launch deadlines.

To Space and Beyond

The highlight of my time with the team was the development of the "Bunny" board (pictured). This board was integrated into the satellite payload and successfully launched into space in January 2023. It features multiple redundant SD card interfaces and robust power management circuitry designed to survive the harsh environment of low Earth orbit.
