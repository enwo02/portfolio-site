Zero to Hero (in One Semester)

I joined the EPFL Spacecraft Team because let's face it: building satellites is cooler than standard homework. Just one semester later, I stepped up as the Team Lead for the On-Board-Computer! I led the development of the architectural prototype—the critical precursor that proved our concepts. Leading the team through these foundational phases was a crash course in engineering leadership that paved the way for the final flight model.

Meet Bunny 🐰

Building on those foundations, the team developed "Bunny"—our explosive debut in space! Realized in a frantic four-month sprint, Bunny is the flight-ready On-Board-Computer built with standard consumer components—proving you don't need military-grade hardware to go to orbit.

Hitching a ride on D-Orbit's ION SCV-009 spacecraft, Bunny has been beaming back data on component reliability since early 2023. The lessons we're learning from this little pioneer are already laying the groundwork for the architecture of future CHESS satellites.
