Predictive rail switch heating algorithm

Python Physics Modeling Optimization Energy Efficiency

The Challenge

Rail switches require heating to remain operational in freezing conditions. However, traditional heating systems often operate inefficiently, consuming significant power even when not strictly necessary. The goal was to optimize this energy consumption without compromising the reliability of the rail network.

Physics-Based Prediction

I developed a physics model of the railroad infrastructure to predict how rail temperature changes over time. By looking ahead a couple of hours, the algorithm determines exactly when heating is required to maintain switch availability. This proactive approach ensures the switch is heated only when necessary, avoiding wasteful energy usage during borderline conditions.

Pilot Success & Impact

The algorithm was implemented in a pilot project where it demonstrated a large percentage reduction in power consumption. Its success proved that predictive heating is a viable path forward for sustainable rail infrastructure.

  • Energy Savings: Significant reduction in power usage compared to traditional control methods.
  • Government Funding: The project's success and potential impact secured government funding for further development.

Smart Load Management

Beyond energy savings, the algorithm also improves load management. Effective distribution of the electrical load is critical for the "Taktfahrplan" (interval timetable). By predicting heating needs, the system can distribute the load more intelligently, preventing peak demand spikes and ensuring smoother grid operation.
